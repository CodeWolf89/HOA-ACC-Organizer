# HOA ACC Organizer Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 13, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 13, 2026

HOA ACC Organizer is a local-first application developed by Christopher Mcmahon-Sutton for organizing homeowners-association Architectural Control Committee (ACC) applications, violation records, supporting photographs and PDFs, reminders, audit history, backups, and nearby-device synchronization.

## 1. Information the app works with

Depending on how an HOA or authorized user uses the app, HOA ACC Organizer can store or process information such as:

- lot numbers and property addresses;
- homeowner or resident names;
- telephone numbers and email addresses;
- rental-unit and billing-address information;
- violation descriptions, governing-rule references, status, notes, dates, and photographs;
- ACC application details, neighbor acknowledgements, review notes, decisions, supporting photographs, and original application PDFs;
- local account usernames, authorization roles, login history, and audit-log entries;
- nearby-device names and synchronization metadata needed to identify compatible devices on the local network.

The app is intended for authorized HOA/ACC use. The HOA or organization using the app is responsible for deciding what member or property information it enters, imports, exports, or shares.

## 2. Local storage

The app stores its working database and managed attachments in the app's local container on the device. Account credentials are verified locally. Password plaintext is not stored by the app. Biometric login uses Apple's Local Authentication and Keychain services; HOA ACC Organizer does not receive or store a Face ID or Touch ID biometric template.

## 3. Camera and photo library

If the user chooses to do so, the app can use the camera or selected photos to document violations or ACC applications. The app does not scan the user's entire photo library for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

## 4. PDF recognition and Apple Intelligence

ACC application PDF recognition is performed locally using Apple PDF/Vision technologies. On supported devices where Apple Intelligence and the Foundation Models framework are available, the app can use Apple's on-device system language model to help organize recognized OCR text. If that capability is unavailable, the app falls back to its deterministic Vision OCR workflow. HOA ACC Organizer does not send ACC application text to a developer-operated AI or cloud service.

## 5. Nearby-device synchronization

The app can discover compatible devices using Bonjour and Apple's Network framework and can transfer HOA records and attachments directly over the local network. Nearby sync does not use a developer-operated cloud database. Users should use nearby synchronization only with trusted devices and trusted local networks.

## 6. Imports, exports, backups, PDFs, and sharing

Users can import structured HOA data and can export or share backups, JSON, violation PDF reports, and ACC decision letters. Exported files can contain personal or property information. Once a user saves or shares an exported file to Files, iCloud Drive, email, messaging, another app, or another storage provider, that destination's privacy and retention practices apply.

## 7. Notifications

If the user grants notification permission, the app can schedule local reminders for violation correction dates, escalation follow-up, ACC application review, and sync staleness. Reminder scheduling is handled on the device.

## 8. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The app does not include advertising SDKs, third-party analytics SDKs, cross-app tracking, or a developer-operated telemetry service. It does not sell personal information or use HOA records for advertising.

Apple may independently process App Store, TestFlight, crash, or diagnostic information according to Apple's own privacy settings and policies. That processing is provided by Apple rather than by an analytics SDK embedded in HOA ACC Organizer.

## 9. Data sharing

HOA ACC Organizer does not automatically send HOA records to the developer. Data can leave the device only through user- or administrator-directed workflows such as nearby sync, imports/exports, backups, PDF sharing, or other system share-sheet destinations. The app does not share HOA data with advertising networks or data brokers.

If users choose to send information to a third party through another app or service, that third party's privacy terms apply. Organizations using HOA ACC Organizer should ensure that any recipient receiving HOA data provides appropriate protection for that data.

## 10. Retention and deletion

Local records remain on a device until they are removed through available app workflows, replaced/restored through an authorized data operation, or the app's local data is deleted. The SuperUser role can remove violation and ACC application records with an audit reason. Removing the app from a device generally removes its app-container data, subject to Apple platform behavior and any device backups.

Files previously exported or shared remain wherever the user saved or sent them until they are deleted from those destinations.

For correction or deletion of HOA member/property information that the organization controls, contact the HOA or application administrator responsible for that data. Privacy questions can also be directed through the Support URL shown on the App Store product page. Do not post private HOA member data in a public GitHub issue.

## 11. Permissions and choices

Users can control camera, photo-library, notification, Face ID, and local-network permissions through Apple system settings. Denying an optional permission can limit the feature that depends on it but does not authorize the app to bypass that choice.

## 12. Children

HOA ACC Organizer is an administrative HOA records tool and is not designed or directed for use by children.

## 13. Security

The app uses Apple platform sandboxing, local database storage, Keychain/Local Authentication for biometric account restoration, password hashing/derivation, content hashes for attachments and transfers, and role-based controls. Users and organizations should protect their devices, passwords, exported files, backups, and local networks from unauthorized access.

## 14. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's data practices change. The effective date and last-updated date above will be revised when material changes are published.
